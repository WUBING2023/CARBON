# Historical external-source retrieval routes

These are the exact routes recorded by the frozen experiment manifests. Their present online availability was not retested while preparing this local candidate. They are external measured/reference data routes, not a public CARBON full-weight archive.

- ClinVar: the saved official NCBI snapshot is GRCh38, release 2026-10-11. The original source URL is https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pub/clinvar/vcf_GRCh38/clinvar_20261011.vcf.gz . Expected bytes: 200437060; SHA256: 05d9481dd7217cc4df15803362d4a4c9eae5875b27b1750867d6bfd2643b3d10. Check paper/source_data/S26_disease_annotations/clinvar/CLINVAR_SNAPSHOT_MANIFEST.json before retrieving it. Retained exact source VCF records and header/MD5 metadata used by this paper are included as small files; the full VCF is not in Git.
- RNAcompete: the measured 420-experiment Z-score archive comes from the author-maintained morrislab/RBPbinding tree b67d8fba065b3c010da672a4980c4ed440745710, path Data/Zscores_420_origrncmpt.tar.gz. The pinned raw URL, author Git blob identity and SHA256 bf65e4bdd12fb1f8fd5db585e5165db24cc1dba6a1b2708f2cc0e12255a676bd are recorded in research/experiments/E09_external_preferences/source_packet/sources/RNAcompete_provenance.json. Use that pinned source, rather than the JPLE prediction files in the same external repository. The candidate includes that original compressed matrix as one fixed 51,769,595-byte soft-limit exception, original construct/assay metadata and all frozen derived Full/Sequence/frequency results. The 115,308,739-byte TXT is verified by scripts/extract_rnacompete_source.py and may be expanded outside Git.
- ENCODE RBNS: original released source accessions, object URLs, MD5 checks and failed/revoked alternatives are preserved in the same experiment's SOURCES.md and sources/ records. Follow the recorded accession/construct identity; do not replace a missing PTBP1 source with another RBP.

All omitted experiment files have original SHA/size/archive-relative records in research/catalog/EXTERNAL_ASSETS.csv. OVERSIZED_EXTERNAL_ASSETS.csv isolates files excluded by the 50,000,000-byte candidate limit. Neural prediction tar archives and full CARBON/comparator weights have no independently verified public download route in this package; obtain the exact hashed local source through a separately authorized archive. Do not silently substitute a newer source release or an ablation checkpoint.
