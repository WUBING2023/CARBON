# Scientific and reproducibility limits

- Historical CARBON/iDeepB/RBPNet scores used different peak/tie/scale conventions. The archived configuration lineage includes both 0/0 and 4/12 thresholds; the package does not assert one shared threshold for the entire benchmark. Figure 2 remains a historical comparison; a fair unified benchmark has not been completed.
- The 222 ablation tasks are not biological replicates. NoGRU and no-multiscale alter capacity. Component prediction value is not proof of a functional mechanism.
- Figure 4 uses 1,851 eligible PTBP1 pairs, 659 genes and 865 cell–gene units, aggregating within gene then equally over cells. All architecture controls retain conservation and global SE; this is allocation evidence, not conservation-specific mechanism evidence.
- Figure 5 RNAcompete retains 25 primary proteins and the complete Full/Sequence/frequency results. Full improves on frequency but not consistently on Sequence. Full and Sequence training histories are not fully matched. Assays, cells, words and technical seeds are not independent proteins.
- The disease annotation subset contains eight variants/13 events, with development exposure: Full direction matches 7/13, Sequence 10/13. ClinVar/TCGA annotations are not binding measurements or disease-causality labels. Reporter evidence retains all 11 variants/66 measurements and selection rules.
- Six-RNA functional maps and FR1–FR4 include negative/mixed outcomes. Unexecuted proposals are not completed experiments. ASB-positive-only events cannot support a variant-detection AUROC.
- Original code association, checksum integrity, frozen arithmetic, fresh model reproduction and independent biological validation are different evidence levels. Early per-run source identities and full raw-data hashes are incomplete.

The archive's fuller historical gap record is research/audit/KNOWN_GAPS.md. This candidate preserves those limitations and adds package-specific installation, external-input and manuscript-binding status.
