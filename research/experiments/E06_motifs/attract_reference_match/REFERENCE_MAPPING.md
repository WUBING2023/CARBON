## Post hoc case selection and full denominators

reporter loci represented by the frozen data (ACVR1B, PDXK, SLC39A13). For each
Full/Sequence direction-discordance category, select the alphabetically first
gene. This yields ACVR1B (Full agrees, Sequence disagrees) and SLC39A13 (reverse).

are training loci. All 24 binding events, 11 reporter loci and 66 author
biological measurements remain in the copied frozen inputs and v25 companion.

author's Figure 6c sheet, not Figure 5e. The common reference is a layout
reference; each plotted measurement keeps its own actual source identity.
