**Figure 4. Recurrent processing and relative signal allocation at repeated short cores.** (a) Native LEPR (K562) and OCRL (HepG2) windows show the stored eCLIP and size-matched input signals, Full, NoGRU and near-capacity local-position model (Local+pos) profile probabilities, and PhyloP at common coordinates. Gray intervals identify two separated inclusive 11-position supports centered on identical CTCT short cores, labeled 1 and 2. Eligible centers lie at positions 5–95; the first two instances passing the sequence-order greedy requirement of at least 12 nt between centers define the pair. T is the stored DNA-alphabet encoding of RNA U; native model inputs were unchanged. Full, NoGRU and Local+pos share probability-axis limits within each window; measured and input traces retain their own scales. Examples are explicitly post hoc: the pair nearest the median error improvement among 340 Full-correct/NoGRU-wrong pairs (LEPR), and nearest the median deterioration among 149 reverse-transition pairs (OCRL). They were locked before Local+pos predictions and were not reselected for that control. They are illustrations, rather than independent validation or extremal favorable cases. Two earlier hash-selected counterexamples remain in the fixed-counterexample supplementary candidate. (b) Site-1 shares conditional on mass in the two supports. The observed share is input-adjusted: each local stored signal mass is corrected by total-window eCLIP mass times its local input fraction. The predicted share uses the three-seed mean native probability profile before taking the ratio. Plotted shares were recomputed from panel-a arrays and matched the full table within 10⁻¹⁰. A share is neither binding probability nor attribution score. (c) Within-cell gene-mean squared-share error differences, with one point per real GENCODE task-gene unit and diamonds for cell means. PTBP1 compares Full with Local+pos (1,851 locus pairs; 659 unique genes; 412 K562 and 453 HepG2 gene units); HNRNPK compares Full with original NoGRU (1,418 pairs; 527 unique genes; 300 and 388 gene units). Every plotted point is inside the displayed range. The PTBP1 mean weights genes equally within cell and cells equally; its displayed interval uses 10,000 paired joint-gene cluster bootstrap replicates and Bonferroni correction for the two registered main contrasts. Observed ties remain in error. Cell gene sets overlap and seeds are not biological replicates. Full has 100,826 parameters; Local+pos has 100,871 and includes pointwise local-feature-by-position interactions and a trained profile bias, retaining global shared SE. Original NoGRU has 44,698 parameters. HNRNPK did not receive a near-capacity position control and does not inherit PTBP1's control result. Chromosomes chr1/X/Y were withheld from matched training and validation but have prior study exposure. The experiment evaluates measured native allocation, without proving conservation-specific or downstream functional correctness.
