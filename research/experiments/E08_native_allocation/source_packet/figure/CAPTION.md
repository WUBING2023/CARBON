Recurrent-path comparison for relative support at repeated PTBP1 short cores.** (a) Native 101-nt windows in LEPR and OCRL show eCLIP signal, size-matched input, Full and NoGRU native profile probabilities, and PhyloP at common coordinates. Gray intervals are the two inclusive 11-position supports around repeated CTCT cores; labels 1 and 2 identify them. T denotes the stored DNA-alphabet input encoding of RNA U. The two examples are explicitly post hoc illustrations: the native pair nearest the median error improvement among the 340 Full-correct/NoGRU-wrong pairs (LEPR), and nearest the median deterioration among the 149 reverse-transition pairs (OCRL). They were fixed before the new position-capacity control predictions; they are neither independent validation nor extremal best cases. The earlier two input-hash-selected counterexamples remain in FIXED_COUNTEREXAMPLES.*. (b) Site-1 fractions conditional on mass in the two supports, using input-adjusted observed signal and the three-seed mean predicted native profile. Shares are recomputed directly from the arrays in panel a and match the full results table within 1e-10. Full and NoGRU have the same probability-axis limits within each example; eCLIP and input retain their own signal scales. Fractions are not binding probabilities or attribution scores. (c) Squared-share error differences averaged within each real GENCODE gene and cell; points are task-gene units and diamonds cell means. All 1,851 locus representatives map to 659 unique genes (412 K562 and 453 HepG2 gene units), with equal gene weight within cell and equal cell weight. The mean difference and 95% interval use 10,000 paired joint-gene cluster bootstrap replicates. Cell gene sets overlap; seeds are not biological replicates. Observed ties remain in error. Full has 100,826 versus NoGRU 44,698 parameters, so capacity and possible absolute-position priors prevent unique sequence-context attribution. The registered near-equal-capacity local position control is pending and absent here. Model-heldout chromosomes chr1/X/Y were exposed in earlier analyses; this is not a completely blind biological study.
