**Candidate Figure. Recurrent-path comparison for relative support at repeated PTBP1 short cores.** (a) Two prespecified native 101-nt windows show eCLIP signal, size-matched input, Full and NoGRU native profile probabilities, and PhyloP at common window coordinates. Gray intervals are the two original 11-position measurement supports around repeated CTCT cores; labels 1 and 2 identify them. Examples were fixed by the first input-identity hash within the earlier measurable, gene-qualified long-gap cohort in each cell, before any new NoGRU predictions. They are counterexamples, not favorable showcases: Full has greater share error in both. FOXD2-AS1 has only four stored signal units in the two supports. (b) Fractions at site 1 conditional on mass in the two supports, using input-adjusted observed signal and the three-seed mean predicted native profile. These fractions are not binding probabilities or attribution scores. (c) Paired squared-share error differences averaged within each real GENCODE gene and cell; each point is a task-gene unit, diamonds are cell means. The combined 1,851 locus-representative pairs map to 659 unique genes (412 K562 and 453 HepG2 gene units), with equal gene weight within cell and equal cell weight. The overall mean difference and 95% interval use 10,000 paired joint-gene cluster bootstrap replicates. The two cell gene sets overlap, and seeds are not independent biological replicates. Observed ties remain in error. Full has 100,826 versus NoGRU 44,698 parameters; the added capacity and possible absolute-position priors prevent unique attribution to sequence context. A separately registered near-equal-capacity local model with explicit learnable position features is pending and is deliberately absent here. Model-heldout chromosomes are chr1/X/Y; these ranges were exposed during prior analyses, so this is not an entirely blind biological confirmation.
