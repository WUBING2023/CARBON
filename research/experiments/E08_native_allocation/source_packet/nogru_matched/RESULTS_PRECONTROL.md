Repeated copies of an identical short PTBP1 core provide a direct test of how the model distributes local support when core identity alone cannot distinguish two positions. We used the first two nonoverlapping CTCT instances in each native 101-nt window, retained the original measured eCLIP and size-matched input profiles, and compared the fraction of support assigned to the first instance conditional on the two 11-position neighborhoods. This is an observational profile-allocation endpoint, rather than a binding probability, physical contact map or experimentally validated attribution. The strict input-adjusted cohort contained 1,851 task-local locus representatives mapping uniquely to 659 real GENCODE genes across K562 and HepG2; 72 equal observed shares remained in the error endpoint. Identical CTCT strings do not guarantee equal complete-motif affinity, so the experiment asks whether the model distinguishes two repeated short cores in their native surroundings.

Including the recurrent pathway reduced gene-equal paired-share squared error from 0.115116 in NoGRU to 0.079884 in Full, a 30.6% relative reduction (paired difference -0.035232; 95% joint-gene bootstrap CI -0.042057 to -0.028577). Ranking accuracy increased from 66.63% to 76.74%, with the same direction in both cells and all three training seeds. The effect persisted for raw eCLIP shares and after excluding the recorded previously exposed genes/loci. The all-gene distribution, rather than the illustrative windows, supplies the population evidence (candidate Fig. c). Native LEPR and OCRL windows illustrate a corrected and a worsened ordering, respectively (candidate Fig. a,b): they were selected post hoc by the median error improvement in the two decision-transition groups, not as extreme favorable cases. Two earlier input-hash-selected counterexamples, FOXD2-AS1 and KRBOX4, remain in the fixed-counterexample supplementary candidate. These failures show that an average model advantage does not mean every footprint is recovered.

Simple input-only ordering rules performed less well: center proximity, local pyrimidine proportion, local PhyloP, GC and input signal achieved approximately 52.4%, 57.7%, 50.6%, 45.3% and 51.7% gene-equal ranking accuracy, respectively. Two unfit symmetric Gaussian position profiles with fixed widths 15 and 25 nt yielded squared-share errors of 0.221614 and 0.150381. In an explicitly post hoc position diagnostic, the 195 pairs from 150 genes whose two core centers differed by at most 5 nt in distance from the window center retained a Full-NoGRU difference of -0.032387 (Bonferroni-3 CI -0.054209 to -0.014789). Exact equal-distance and at-most-2-nt subsets were smaller and their intervals crossed zero. These diagnostics weaken a simple symmetric center-preference explanation but do not exclude richer absolute-position effects.

The original Full-NoGRU comparison also changes parameter count (100,826 versus 44,698). Therefore it presently establishes a pathway-associated improvement in local profile judgment, not unique GRU causality, sequence-context causality, conservation-specific explanation or downstream functional correctness. A registered control with 100,871 parameters, pointwise position-conditioned features and an explicit learnable position bias is being trained at the same complete budget; no pending result has been inserted into the candidate figure. The earlier Full-versus-Sequence/Mean/Perm conservation comparisons and their inconclusive simultaneous intervals remain unchanged. These experiments use model-heldout chromosomes but reuse a previously exposed study domain.
