## Discriminating question and bounded answer

Do the retained CARBON outputs recover external sequence-order preferences beyond native abundance, and do such preferences extend consistently to measured allele ordering in the already exposed natural-SNV cohort? Alternative explanations include frequency proxies, unmatched Full/Sequence training, selected favorable examples and fixed-input perturbation effects. c directly retains frequency-opposed external-first edits, all proteins, both model responses and the full-word primary endpoint. e shows every eligible allele event on one non-outcome ordering, with observed direction, fixed Phi and all model controls. Favorable examples are not a sufficient answer; negative/mixed event responses and the lack of general Full-minus-Sequence superiority remain explicit.
