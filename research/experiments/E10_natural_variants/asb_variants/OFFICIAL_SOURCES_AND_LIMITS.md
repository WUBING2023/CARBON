## Immutable-source and statistical limits

The prior 7,262-event cohort and all model outputs were already inspected. The 396-event chromosome subset is retained for a clearly bounded descriptive display, with the full exposed cohort supplied separately. Annotation success cannot select model-performance subsets. The paper's existing source data S19 remain the authoritative original paper-download provenance; Data S25 supplies the compact byte-exact processed inputs needed for this extension.

The event rows are clustered within RNA-region/locus groups and RBPs. Macro direction credits average events within task, tasks within RBP, then RBPs equally. RBP-resampled direction intervals and paired gene/RBP positive-multiplier sensitivity are both retained. The primary adjusted rank-slope intervals are **first-order two-way multiplier approximations**, explicitly deviating from the original frozen full-refit plan. A separately seeded, bounded 200-replicate true weighted nuisance-refit sensitivity and its region-adjusted extension verify that the strict-subset intervals still span zero. No endpoint or subset changed after those checks.

Central raw PhyloP is the reference-base evolutionary score. The same reference conservation profile is supplied to both alleles; an alternative allele's evolutionary history is not measured or recomputed. Conditional controls cover task, strand, local sequence bases, GC, mutation class, raw depth and source REF preference. An independent cognate RBP motif score and an independent quantitative assay-effect magnitude are not available. The adjusted relationships remain exploratory and do not identify a causal conservation contribution.

Fixed-input flat/permuted conservation responses use the existing Full checkpoint; they are input-sensitivity experiments, not retrained channel ablations. Full and Sequence historically trained models are not known to be perfectly matched. A higher conservation association for Full would not, by itself, prove causal information gain. The full-development-cohort Full magnitude association is retained despite its absence of a stable strict-subset counterpart. All complete and unsuccessful rows remain available.

The threshold audit compares 1e−7 with 1e−6 only inside the current frozen 7,262-event prediction package. It does not equate this archive with every earlier S1.14 prediction array. The S1.14 report from its separately archived arrays is left unchanged.
