Exact clinical and tumor annotations in the complete existing variant population.
a, Annotation availability among all 4,669 unique GRCh38 variants: 520 exact
ClinVar matches and six exact open TCGA matches. Missing/unmatched records remain
distinct from benign classification. b, Every 13 ASB event at the union of three
ClinVar P/LP and six TCGA variants (eight distinct variants): exact site, RBP/cell,
database flag, source allele preference and Full/Sequence ALT-minus-REF response.
Clinical and tumor flags can overlap. Shared asinh horizontal axis has linear
width 0.02 and original-unit ticks. All measured source preferences are REF;
11 events belong to training chromosomes and two to validation chromosomes.
Current VCV assertions and tumor occurrence are not new binding measurements.
c, All 520 exact ClinVar variants' reference PhyloP by aggregate germline class,
including no germline class, conflicts and uncertainty. Every point is a unique
variant; bars are descriptive medians, not confidence intervals. The three P/LP
sites are highlighted by name. d, All 428 available current ClinVar TGP ALT
frequencies versus reference PhyloP, without pooling the separate earlier
allele-count-based NCBI frequencies. Zero, when explicitly reported, is plotted;
unavailable AF remains missing. The single annotated clinical site with TGP AF,
ASL, is highlighted. No class-discrimination performance, significance test,
disease mechanism or mutant evolutionary score is inferred. Snapshot versions,
complete statuses/conditions, exact-source AF and all source events are in Data S26.
